Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

This page describes what information we collect when you visit fletchaero.com or contact us, and how we handle it. We keep this minimal — we don't run advertising, we don't sell data, and we don't want to hold anything we don't need.

What we collect

  • Contact information you send us. When you email us at contact@unmannd.com, we receive your email address, your message and any files or details you choose to include.
  • Basic server logs. Our host (GitHub Pages) records standard access information — IP address, browser type, pages requested and timestamp — for the purpose of operating the site and preventing abuse.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels are set by this site at this time.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry.
  • To keep the site operating and secure.
  • We do not share the information you send with third parties for their own use, and we do not sell it.

How long we keep it

We retain contact correspondence for as long as it is useful to the enquiry and, after that, for a reasonable period to keep a record of the conversation. You may ask us to delete your correspondence at any time and we will do so unless we are required by law to keep it.

Your rights

You may write to us to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We aim to respond within 30 days. Write to contact@unmannd.com with the subject line "Privacy request".

Children

This site is intended for a professional and adult audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above. We recommend reviewing this page from time to time.

Contact

For any question about this policy, email contact@unmannd.com.
