    Engineering

    Built to keep flying when something doesn't.

    At altitude with a load on, there is no margin for a single point of failure. Everything that could strand the aircraft has a second path.

    01

    Triple-redundant IMU

    Three independent inertial units voting on attitude. A drifting sensor is outvoted, not followed.

    02

    Dual RTK GPS

    Centimetre-class positioning, with a second receiver carrying the mission if the first degrades.

    03

    Mag-independent heading

    Heading estimation that does not rely on the magnetic field — the failure mode that ends most heavy-lift flights near steel and high current.

    04

    Four independent batteries

    Separate packs on separate paths. Losing one is a degraded sortie, not a falling aircraft.

    05

    LiDAR altimetry

    True height above ground for terrain-referenced flight, low-level work and precise set-down on unprepared surfaces.

    06

    Hybrid airframe construction

    Aluminium structural frame, carbon-fibre arms and carbon boom tubes — stiffness where the loads are, light weight where they are not.

    07

    Protected avionics enclosure

    Rated −20 °C to 45 °C. Dust, damp and thin cold air are operating conditions, not exceptions.

    08

    Rapid payload integration

    A common mechanical, power and data interface, so a new payload becomes a supported configuration rather than an engineering project.
